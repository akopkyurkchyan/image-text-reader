Image to Text turns photos into editable text in seconds.

Take a picture with your camera or choose an image from your gallery, and the app extracts the text using fast on-device OCR. Edit the result, then copy it to your clipboard.

Features:
• Take a photo or pick from gallery
• On-device text recognition — your images stay on your phone
• Edit extracted text before copying
• Simple, focused interface

Perfect for digitizing notes, receipts, signs, documents, and screenshots.

Image to Text does not require an account. Text recognition runs locally on your device using Google ML Kit.
